ESL Lesson: Research

Understanding Research

Learning how to ask the right questions and find the best answers.

What is Research?

Research is a careful way of finding facts and answering questions about something you don't know.

Part 1 of Research

Study

To look closely at books, articles, or data to learn information.

Part 2 of Research

Investigate

To search for facts and evidence, just like a detective solving a mystery!

Part 3 of Research

Discover

To find out something completely new that you didn't know before.

Why do we do Research?

🌎 To understand the world
💡 To solve big problems
🗣️ To prove an argument
🚀 To invent new things

The First Step of Research

STEP 1

Ask a Question

Everything starts with a good question. You cannot find answers if you don't know what you are looking for!

What makes a
GOOD
research question?

It needs to follow 3 important rules...

Rule #1

It must be CLEAR

Easy to understand. The words are simple, and everyone knows exactly what you are asking.

Rule #2

It must be FOCUSED

Specific. It is not too broad (about everything) and not too narrow (about just one tiny detail).

Rule #3

It must be COMPLEX

It needs explanation. You cannot answer it with just "Yes" or "No". You have to do real research to answer it!

Is this a good research question?

"Do dogs bark?"

No!

  • It can be answered with "Yes".
  • It is not complex.
  • No research is needed.

"Why do domestic dogs bark at strangers?"

Yes!

  • It requires reading and studying.
  • It is focused (strangers).
  • It is complex (needs reasons).
