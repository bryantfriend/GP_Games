Solubility Science!

Learn how things dissolve in water, and how temperature changes everything.

1. What is Solubility?

Have you ever mixed sugar into tea or water?

Solubility is a measure of how much of a substance can dissolve in a liquid.

2. Important Words

Solute

The stuff that gets dissolved (like sugar or salt).

+

Solvent

The liquid that does the dissolving (like water).

Solute + Solvent = Solution

4. Can you dissolve unlimited amounts?

What happens if you keep adding more and more salt to a glass of water?

→ Add MORE →

Eventually, the salt stops dissolving! It just piles up at the bottom. The water is "full".

5. Saturated Solutions

Saturated Solution

A solution that contains the MAXIMUM amount of solute that can dissolve.

It cannot hold any more! If you add more, it will not dissolve.

An Unsaturated Solution is one that can still dissolve more stuff.

7. The Power of Heat

How does Temperature change solubility?

Cold Water (20°C)

Dissolves a little sugar

→

Hot Water (80°C)

Dissolves A LOT of sugar

For most solids (like sugar or salt), increasing the temperature INCREASES solubility.

8. Why does Hot Water dissolve more?

Hot water molecules have more energy and move faster.

They crash into the solid faster and break it apart easier. More space opens up in the liquid for the solute to hide!

10. The Solubility Curve

Scientists use a graph to show exactly how much solute dissolves at different temperatures. It is called a Solubility Curve.

Notice how the line goes UP as the temperature goes RIGHT (gets hotter).

11. How to Read the Curve

Example: Look at 50°C on the bottom.

Follow the red dotted line UP to the green curve.

Look LEFT to the side. It says 50g.

So, at 50°C, you can dissolve 50g of Chemical X in 100g of water.

14. Is it Saturated?

The line on the graph represents the MAXIMUM (Saturated).

Any point BELOW the line is an Unsaturated solution. It has room for more solute!
